Creature Codex

A visual guide to the creatures of your favorite franchises, starting with Digimon and Pokémon.

Each week we cover one evolution line from each franchise: a Digimon partner from Fresh to Mega, and a Pokémon family from first stage to final form, Mega Evolution and Gigantamax included. Every video shows types, stats, debut and how each evolution happens, with 3D models and clear on-screen text you can pause and read at your own pace. There is no narration.

Sources

Our facts come from public reference sites such as Wikimon, Digimon Wiki, Bulbapedia and Serebii, and are written in our own words. The sources for each video are listed in its description.
